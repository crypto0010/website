Our Specialties

Comprehensive Services

From minimally invasive arthroscopy to complex joint reconstructions — a full spectrum of orthopedic treatments powered by the latest technology.

01

Knee Surgery

Our knee surgery program encompasses the full range of procedures — from total and partial knee replacement to complex revision surgeries. We use computer-assisted navigation systems that achieve alignment accuracy within 1 degree.

Patients benefit from rapid recovery protocols, same-day mobilization, and personalized rehabilitation plans that get them back on their feet faster.

Total Knee Replacement
Partial Knee Replacement
Revision Surgery
Computer Navigation
02

ACL Reconstruction

Our arthroscopic ACL reconstruction program uses anatomical single-bundle and double-bundle techniques with autograft or allograft options. We specialize in helping athletes return to competitive sports safely.

Combined with our ABFT partnership, athletes receive comprehensive care from injury through return-to-sport, including sport-specific rehabilitation protocols.

Single Bundle ACL
Double Bundle ACL
Multi-Ligament Repair
Return-to-Sport Program
03

Shoulder Surgery

Our shoulder surgery program includes rotator cuff repair, shoulder replacement (anatomic and reverse), labral repair, and stabilization procedures — all performed using the latest minimally invasive techniques.

We also offer non-surgical treatment options including PRP therapy, guided injections, and structured physiotherapy programs for patients who may not need surgery.

Rotator Cuff Repair
Shoulder Replacement
Bankart Repair
Frozen Shoulder
