About Galaxy Hospital

Our Story

Founded with a singular vision — to make world-class orthopedic care accessible to all in Central India and beyond.

Our Mission

Redefining orthopedic excellence

Galaxy Hospital Bhopal was established with the belief that every patient deserves access to cutting-edge orthopedic treatment, regardless of background. Our team of fellowship-trained surgeons combines international expertise with deep local commitment.

We continuously invest in the latest surgical technologies, from computer-navigated joint replacement systems to HD arthroscopy platforms, ensuring our patients receive the same quality of care available at the world's leading medical institutions.

Our Vision

Building the future of healthcare

Our vision extends beyond surgical excellence. We aim to build a comprehensive orthopedic ecosystem — from prevention and early diagnosis through surgery and complete rehabilitation.

Through our partnership with the Abhinav Bindra Foundation, we also contribute to the development of India's sporting talent, providing specialized sports medicine and rehabilitation services to athletes at every level.
